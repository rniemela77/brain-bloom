Ether Trace

A tiny workout for your imagination.

About

Short exercises for your imagination.

Ether Trace helps you practice coming up with ideas. Each exercise takes a few minutes.

Right now there are two. Random Connection gives you unrelated words and asks how they could be connected. What Else Could It Be? gives you an ordinary object and asks what else you could use it for.

Linking things that don’t usually go together is a basic part of creative thinking. The more you do it, the easier it gets.

No account. No score. More exercises later.

Exercise 01

Random Connection

Generate new ideas by connecting ones that don’t usually go together.

Come up with 3 ways these could be connected.

Why it works

It trains the brain to make unusual associations — the basic building block of a lot of creative thinking.
