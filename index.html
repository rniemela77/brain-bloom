About

Short exercises for your imagination.

Ether Trace helps you practice coming up with ideas. Each exercise takes a few minutes.

Right now there is one: Random Connection. You get unrelated words, like umbrella and volcano. You think of three ways they could be connected, and you write them down.

Linking things that don’t usually go together is a basic part of creative thinking. The more you do it, the easier it gets.

No account. No score. More exercises later.

It trains the brain to make unusual associations — the basic building block of a lot of creative thinking.
